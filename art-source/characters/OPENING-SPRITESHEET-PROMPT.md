# Prompt aset Malin Opening (membawa buntalan)

Create ONE production-ready 2D character sprite sheet only, not a concept board.

- Exact layout: 8 columns x 3 rows = 24 equal-sized frames.
- PNG with real transparent alpha background.
- No labels, no text, no numbers, no title, no borders, no panels, no portraits, no separate props, no background scenery.
- Every frame uses the same cell size and the same character scale. Keep the feet on a consistent baseline. Keep the whole character inside every cell with enough transparent padding.
- Side view facing right in every frame; consistent proportions, face, clothing, outline, and lighting.

Character: young Malin from the existing gameplay reference. Tan skin, messy black hair, red-orange patterned Minangkabau headband with tails, gray sleeveless vest over a simple cream shirt, red waist sash, loose brown cropped trousers, bare feet. Add one small beige cloth travel bundle tied over his upper back with a simple shoulder strap. It must read as a traditional cloth bundle, NOT bamboo, NOT a modern backpack, NOT a weapon. Keep the bundle visible behind his shoulder and make it move subtly with his body.

Rows:
1. Frames 0-7: subtle idle/breathing loop while carrying the bundle.
2. Frames 8-15: complete slow walk cycle to the right; clear alternating foot contacts, passing poses, and arm swing; the bundle gently bounces but stays attached.
3. Frames 16-23: cinematic farewell action, in order: slow down, stop, turn head/upper body slightly to look back over his shoulder, raise one hand for a brief goodbye, lower the hand, turn forward, and take the first steps away. This row is a one-shot sequence, not a looping walk.

Keep the art style consistent with the supplied Malin gameplay sprite reference. Do not change Malin's identity or outfit. Do not add the mother to this sheet.
