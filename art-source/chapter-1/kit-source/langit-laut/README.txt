Malin Kundang Chapter 1 — Gunung, Awan, dan Laut

14 PNG files extracted as individual assets from the generated art sheet:
- 8 clouds
- 3 mountain parallax strips
- 1 rocky islet
- 2 ocean tiles

The extracted assets retain RGBA transparency. Cloud and object sprites were enlarged to meet or exceed 2x pack dimensions. Mountain strips use 2x pack canvas sizes with the silhouette aligned to the bottom. Ocean tiles were resized/cropped to the specified 2x pack dimensions.

Important production note:
The generated art is not guaranteed to tile perfectly. Check mountain and ocean left/right seams in the game editor and repair with offset/clone/healing as described in the asset guide. The ocean surface was resized to the specified tile ratio; inspect wave shape before shipping.
